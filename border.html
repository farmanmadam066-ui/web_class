The BMW 3 Series is a luxury compact executive car known for its sporty performance and high-tech features. In India, it is primarily available in Long Wheelbase (LWB) and performance variants like the M340i xDrive. Key Specifications & Pricing (India) • BMW 3 Series Long Wheelbase (330Li M Sport): • Price: Starts at approximately ₹62.60 Lakh to ₹62.70 Lakh (ex-showroom). • Engine: 2.0-litre TwinPower Turbo 4-cylinder petrol engine. • Transmission: 8-speed Steptronic Sport automatic transmission. • Features: Curved display with BMW OS 8.5 (14.9-inch touchscreen and 12.3-inch digital cluster), Harman Kardon 16-speaker audio system, panoramic/electric glass roof, and ambient lighting. • BMW M340i xDrive (Performance Variant): • Price: Starts around ₹77.10 Lakh to ₹77.80 Lakh (ex-showroom). • Engine: 3.0-litre turbocharged inline-6 petrol engine. • Power & Torque: ~368.78 bhp (374 hp) and 500 Nm torque with 4WD (xDrive). • Performance: 0 to 100 km/h in roughly 4.4 seconds with a top speed of 253 km/h.
